Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

English

Airplane Window does not collect any personal data.

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • The app does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos.
  • Your last chosen flight and your unlocked flights are saved only on your device.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive your payment details. Their privacy policies apply to purchases.
  • The app works without the internet. It connects only to the App Store or Google Play to buy or restore purchases.
  • The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect data from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, we will update this page. Questions: ismetkorucuoglu07@gmail.com.
